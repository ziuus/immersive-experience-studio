# Studio Philosophy

A digital experience is a designed encounter between a person and an idea. It has a beginning, atmosphere, rhythm, hierarchy of attention and a memory that remains after the screen is closed.

## Principles
- **Specificity creates originality:** find the particular truth, tension, ritual, ambition or contradiction that belongs to this brand.
- **The medium must earn its place:** 3D, shaders, sound, scroll choreography and generative systems need a clear purpose.
- **Every moment has a job:** establish context, create tone, explain, build trust, reveal, invite exploration or support action.
- **Cohesion beats spectacle:** a strong hero cannot rescue an incoherent experience.
- **Usability enables immersion:** navigation, readability, feedback, performance and accessibility are part of art direction.
- **A pause can be powerful:** not every moment needs movement.

Definition of done: message understood; concept distinctive and coherent; critical journeys work; responsive/accessibility/performance checked to agreed extent; defects resolved or accepted; user approves.
