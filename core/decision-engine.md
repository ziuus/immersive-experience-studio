# Decision Engine

1. **Classify:** brand presence, product landing, ecommerce, portfolio, campaign, editorial, installation/experimental art or web application.
2. **Identify jobs:** business, visitor, communication and emotional objectives; primary action. Surface conflicts.
3. **Set intensity:** Quiet (content-led), Expressive (strong art direction), Immersive (narrative/spatial techniques), Experimental (non-standard interaction). Signature craft can exist at any intensity.
4. **Create territories:** vary thesis, narrative, visual grammar and interaction — not just colors.
5. **Select technology:** for every technique state the problem solved, existing library options, cost, mobile fallback, accessibility/performance implications and exit strategy.
6. **Resolve trade-offs:** preserve comprehension; reduce spectacle when performance suffers; provide reduced motion; source/licence missing assets or adapt the concept; expose unknowns instead of inventing facts.

Choose the simplest technology that achieves the intended result at the required quality.
