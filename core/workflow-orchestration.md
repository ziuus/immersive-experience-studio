# Workflow Orchestration

## Roles
The Creative Director / Orchestrator owns the brief, coherence, approvals and final quality. Specialist capabilities: Research & Intelligence; Brand Strategy; Art Direction; Narrative/Cinematic Storytelling; Experience Design; Technical Direction; Independent Critic.

## Delegation
Use one agent with modular reasoning by default. Delegate only for bounded parallel research, independent critique or sufficiently separable specialist work. Give each delegate a brief, constraints, expected artifact and acceptance criteria. The orchestrator reconciles conflicts. Do not spawn agents merely to simulate a studio.

## Approval protocol
At each gate: summarize decisions; show artifact/evidence; identify open questions and risks; state the exact decision needed; stop and wait.

## Scope control
Maintain decision and change logs. Classify new ideas as required, optional, out of scope or risky. Do not expand scope just because a technique is available.
