# Cross-Disciplinary Art Direction

## Role
Translate principles from other creative disciplines into a coherent digital experience. The goal is not to decorate a website with references; it is to borrow ways of thinking about attention, emotion, sequence, space, material and meaning.

## Translation method
For every reference or discipline, identify:
1. The mechanism: what makes the work effective (framing, rhythm, scale, juxtaposition, reveal, negative space, choreography, material contrast, etc.)?
2. The visitor effect: what does it make someone notice, feel, anticipate or understand?
3. The digital translation: what changes in composition, content, pacing, navigation or interaction?
4. The boundary: what should not be copied, and what would become a gimmick online?

## Art-direction dimensions
- Composition: focal point, hierarchy, asymmetry, scale, rhythm, negative space, density and transitions between frames.
- Image direction: subject, lens/perspective, crop, lighting, color grade, movement, sequence and asset provenance.
- Typography: voice, hierarchy, line length, scale, contrast, expressive use and legibility.
- Space: thresholds, depth, orientation, continuity, pacing, wayfinding and exits.
- Material: surface, texture, transparency, reflection, tactility and restraint; use only when meaningful.
- Time: stillness, anticipation, repetition, acceleration, interruption, crescendo and release.
- Participation: what the visitor controls, discovers, contributes or changes; never make interaction mandatory for comprehension.
- Sound: optional, user-initiated, controllable and nonessential to understanding.

## Avoid category defaults
Do not assume luxury means serif + black/gold; technology means dark + neon; creativity means oversized typography; immersive means WebGL; premium means minimal; cinematic means dark + slow motion. Establish the visual logic from the concept and audience.

## Frame test
Before implementation, describe or sketch at least three key frames: first impression, narrative/interaction peak, and conversion/closing moment. They must look and feel like parts of one authored experience. If the frames could be swapped with another brand without anyone noticing, art direction is too generic.
