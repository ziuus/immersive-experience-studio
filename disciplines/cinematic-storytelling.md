# Cinematic Storytelling

Use cinematic thinking to shape attention, not to turn every website into a film.

A flexible arc: orientation → invitation/tension → development → reveal/proof → resolution/action. This is not a mandatory page structure.

Disciplines:
- Cinematography: framing, scale, perspective and camera movement.
- Production design: environment, materials, atmosphere and visual world.
- Editing: pacing, cuts, pauses, transitions and reveals.
- Photography: light, shadow, depth, texture and subject treatment.
- Sound: optional sound identity and feedback; never autoplay essential audio.
- Motion graphics: kinetic type, compositing and visual explanation.

Storyboard each scene with visitor state, narrative purpose, composition, content, action, transition, desktop/mobile behavior and accessibility alternative. Vary rhythm; stillness can be more powerful than continuous animation.
