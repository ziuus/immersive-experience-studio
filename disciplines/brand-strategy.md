# Brand Strategy

Translate business and audience context into a creative problem.

- **Offer:** what is actually provided?
- **Audience:** who is it for, in what situation?
- **Tension:** what problem, desire or barrier exists?
- **Promise:** what outcome can credibly be promised?
- **Proof:** what evidence supports it?
- **Difference:** why choose it over alternatives?
- **Action:** what should visitors do next?

Message hierarchy: primary proposition → explanation → proof → objection handling → action. Define voice through usable dimensions and examples, not adjectives alone. Do not manufacture differentiation; identify honest opportunities in service, process, proof, audience focus or delivery.
