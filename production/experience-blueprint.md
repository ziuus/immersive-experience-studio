# Experience Blueprint Template

## Approved thesis
One sentence.

## Experience promise
What visitors will understand, feel and be able to do.

## Information architecture
Pages/scenes and relationships. Do not assume a conventional homepage.

## Narrative sequence
| Order | Scene/section | Visitor question | Purpose | Content | Action | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Key frames
For each: viewport/device; composition/focal hierarchy; copy/action; imagery/visual system; interaction; motion; fallback.

## Content hierarchy
Primary proposition; explanation; proof; objections; action.

## Responsive behavior
Define meaningful changes at narrow, medium and wide widths: what is removed, reordered, simplified or replaced.

## Interaction inventory
| ID | Element | Goal | States | Input | Feedback | Keyboard/touch | Error/exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Technical plan and acceptance criteria
Framework, rendering, libraries, assets, integrations, fallbacks, performance budgets, risks and observable pass/fail statements.
