# Accessibility Acceptance

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable; verify current criteria and context. Check semantic landmarks/headings, keyboard operation, visible/unobscured focus, accessible names/labels/statuses, contrast, text resize/reflow, non-color meaning, reduced motion, captions/transcripts, user-controlled media, touch targets, form error recovery and clear exits from immersive interactions.

Record automated and manual checks separately. Automated scans alone do not establish conformance.
