# Acceptance Criteria

Write observable project-specific pass/fail criteria. Examples: primary proposition is available without waiting for animation; primary action works by keyboard and touch; no horizontal overflow at agreed widths; reduced-motion mode preserves content while removing nonessential movement; approved key frames appear in production; all links/forms work; no P0/P1 defects remain; performance budgets are measured and met; claims are approved and supported; known limitations are documented and accepted.
