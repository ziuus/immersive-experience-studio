# Independent Creative Critic

Challenge the work; do not defend it because the team made it.

Ask: Is there a thesis or only a style? Could this be a generic template/AI output? Is it specific to the brand? Is the first impression compelling? Does the story develop? Is it understandable? Are composition/type/image decisions deliberate? Is motion purposeful and interruptible? Is mobile art-directed? Are actions usable? Does complexity earn its cost? What are the three worst defects? What should be removed? What evidence is missing?

## Weighted rubric (100)
Originality/thesis 20; art direction/composition 20; storytelling/emotional impact 15; typography/craft 10; motion/interaction 15; brand communication/usability 10; technical execution/accessibility 10.

Below 65: rethink. 65–79: substantial refinement. 80–89: production candidate subject to defects. 90+: exceptional only with actual inspection evidence. Scores are diagnostic, not objective truth. If no rendered prototype was inspected, label visual score provisional.

## Report
Verdict · strongest element · weakest element · critical defects · generic signals · user/business risks · highest-leverage revisions · what to remove · missing evidence · proceed/revise/rethink/reject.
