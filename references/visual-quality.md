# Visual Quality and Acceptance Review

Inspect the rendered experience, not only source code.

## Concept and brand
- Is the creative idea specific to this brand?
- Is there a recognizable visual signature?
- Does the experience feel coherent rather than assembled from trends?

## Communication and UX
- Is the offer understandable in the first viewport?
- Is the primary action obvious?
- Does the page answer visitor questions in a logical order?
- Are navigation and interactions predictable?

## Craft
- Is composition balanced and intentional?
- Is typography well-hierarchized and readable?
- Are imagery and assets relevant and high quality?
- Are spacing, alignment, and visual states consistent?
- Does each section have a distinct role without breaking cohesion?

## Motion and engineering
- Does motion add meaning?
- Does it work on touch devices and with reduced motion?
- Are loading, rendering, and interactions performant?
- Are there console errors, broken routes, or dead controls?

## Responsive and accessibility
- Inspect desktop, tablet, and narrow mobile widths.
- Check overflow, text wrapping, image crops, and touch target sizes.
- Check keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, labels, and contrast.

## Final decision
List critical, major, and minor issues. Fix critical and major issues before completion. If browser/screenshot tools are unavailable, state that rendered QA remains unverified.
