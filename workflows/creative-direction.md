# Stage 1 — Creative Direction

## 1. Understand the assignment

Inspect the supplied brief and, where possible, the current site/repository. Extract:
- Brand/product and what it actually offers.
- Primary audience and their level of familiarity.
- User need, differentiator, proof, and objections.
- Primary business goal and primary CTA.
- Required content, pages, features, and constraints.
- Existing brand assets, identity rules, and technical stack.

Separate facts, assumptions, and open questions. Do not invent missing proof or product capabilities.

## 2. Research with intent

Research relevant creative studios, live websites, interaction examples, visual references, and implementation tools when access is available. Select references for specific reasons (e.g. typography, pacing, image treatment, navigation), not because they are fashionable. Check whether proposed libraries are maintained, compatible, and proportionate to the task. Do not copy layouts or assets.

## 3. Develop three distinct creative territories

Each territory must differ in its underlying idea and visual logic. For each, define:
- Name and one-sentence thesis.
- Emotional tone and brand fit.
- Visual metaphor or central creative device.
- Composition and layout behavior.
- Typography and color direction.
- Image/illustration/3D approach.
- Motion and interaction language.
- How the product/offer remains clear.
- Technical complexity, performance risks, and asset needs.

Avoid presenting superficial variations (such as the same design in three palettes) as distinct concepts.

## 4. Recommend

Choose the strongest territory based on brand fit, audience comprehension, differentiation, feasibility, accessibility, performance, and conversion. Explain trade-offs honestly. Do not claim awards are guaranteed.

## 5. Deliverable and gate

Present a concise Creative Direction Proposal. Include any references and what specifically is being learned from them. Ask for approval or a choice. Do not start full implementation until approved, unless the user explicitly authorizes autonomous progression.
